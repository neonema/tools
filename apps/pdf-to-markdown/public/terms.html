PDF to Markdown is provided as-is to convert the text of a PDF into Markdown in your browser. Files have to be 80 MB or smaller and 400 pages or fewer.

The converter reads the text that is drawn on each page and groups it into lines. A larger font becomes a heading. Bullets and numbers become lists. Two columns are read one after the other. A line repeated at the top or bottom of the page, and a page number in that margin, is left out. Web and email links on the page become Markdown links. You can edit the result before you copy or download it.

Text inside images, such as a scanned page, is not converted. Tables, equations, and rotated text are not converted. A borderless table may come out as ordinary lines. Password-protected PDFs can't be opened.

Match the layout is optional. It lines up columns from the words and positions already read on your device. It does not see a picture of the page. A complex table can still look different from the PDF.

The file is handled locally. We do not store it or the Markdown, and we are not responsible for how you use the result.
