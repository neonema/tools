Privacy Policy

PDF to Markdown at tools.neonema.com turns the text of a PDF into Markdown entirely in your browser on your device. PDFs larger than 80 MB, or longer than 400 pages, are refused before they are read.

The PDF you choose stays on your device. It is not uploaded, and we do not collect, store, or transmit it. The Markdown is created locally. There are no accounts, no cookies set by this tool, no analytics, and no advertising.

Password-protected PDFs are not opened. A password is never sent anywhere, because the file never leaves your device.

This site is served from a content delivery network, which processes standard request data (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver the page — the same as any website you visit. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. The PDF reader is included with this site and runs on your device. No other third-party services are used.
