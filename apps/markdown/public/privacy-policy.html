Privacy Policy

Markdown Preview at tools.neonema.com renders Markdown you type or paste. Rendering runs in your browser on your device.

Your text is never sent to NeoNema servers. We do not collect, store, or transmit what you enter. There are no accounts, no cookies set by this tool, no analytics, and no advertising.

Images in the Markdown are not downloaded. A picture is shown as a link, or as its alt text when the address is not a normal web or email link. The page does not request that image.

Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. This site is served from a content delivery network, which processes standard request data (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver the page — the same as any website you visit. No other third-party services are used.
