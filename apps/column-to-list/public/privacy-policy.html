Privacy Policy

The Column to List tool at tools.neonema.com joins a column of text into a delimited list and splits a list back into lines entirely in your browser on your device.

Your input is never sent to NeoNema servers. We do not collect, store, or transmit what you paste. There are no accounts, no cookies set by this tool, no analytics, and no advertising.

This site is served from a content delivery network, which processes standard request data (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver the page — the same as any website you visit. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. No other third-party services are used.
