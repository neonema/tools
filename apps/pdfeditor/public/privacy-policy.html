Privacy Policy

PDF Editor at tools.neonema.com lets you edit the text and text boxes in a PDF, or add an image such as a signature, entirely in your browser on your device. PDFs larger than 20 MB are refused before they are read.

The PDF and any image you choose stay on your device. They are not uploaded, and we do not collect, store, or transmit them. The edited file is created locally. There are no accounts, no cookies set by this tool, no analytics, and no advertising.

The saved PDF is the document itself. This tool does not add a watermark, and it does not write a NeoNema credit, name, or logo into the file. If you download without changing anything, you get the original file unchanged.

Password-protected PDFs are not opened. A password is never sent anywhere, because the file never leaves your device.

This site is served from a content delivery network, which processes standard request data (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver the page — the same as any website you visit. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. The PDF libraries are included with this page and run on your device. No other third-party services are used.
