Privacy Policy

The Dice roller at tools.neonema.com rolls dice entirely in your browser on your device, using the Web Crypto API.

Each roll stays on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit the number of dice, the die you pick, or the numbers that come up. There are no accounts, no cookies set by this tool, no analytics, and no advertising.

This site is served from a content delivery network, which processes standard request data (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver the page — the same as any website you visit. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. No other third-party services are used.
