Privacy Policy

The Password Generator at tools.neonema.com creates random passwords entirely in your browser on your device, using the Web Crypto API.

Generated passwords are never sent to NeoNema servers. We do not collect, store, or transmit the password or the options you choose. There are no accounts, no cookies set by this tool, no analytics, and no advertising.

If you copy a password, it is placed on your device clipboard by your browser. NeoNema cannot read that clipboard.

This site is served from a content delivery network, which processes standard request data (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver the page — the same as any website you visit. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. No other third-party services are used.
