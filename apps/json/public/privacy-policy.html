Privacy Policy

The JSON Utility Toolkit runs entirely in your browser. JSON you paste, upload, or generate is processed locally on your device and is never sent to NeoNema servers.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no cookies set by this tool, no analytics, and no advertising. We do not store or transmit the content you work with.

Third parties

This site is served from a content delivery network, which processes standard request data (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver the page — the same as any website you visit. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. No other third-party services are used.
