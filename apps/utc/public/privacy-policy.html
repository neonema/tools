Privacy Policy

UTC shows your local time and Coordinated Universal Time using your device clock and the time zone reported by your browser. Daylight saving status is derived from that same local time zone data.

Everything runs in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit your time, time zone, or any other input. There are no accounts, no cookies set by this tool, no analytics, and no advertising.

This site is served from a content delivery network, which processes standard request data (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver the page — the same as any website you visit. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. No other third-party services are used.
