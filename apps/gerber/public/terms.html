Gerber to SVG is provided as-is to turn one Gerber layer, such as solder paste, copper, mask, silk, or outline, into an SVG drawing in your browser.

The drawing is made locally from the file you choose. It shows that one layer. It is not a check that a board is ready to fabricate, and unusual files from older CAD tools may not render completely. We do not store the file or the SVG and are not responsible for how you use the result.
