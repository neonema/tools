Privacy Policy

Encrypt & Decrypt Text at tools.neonema.com locks and unlocks text in your browser, on your device. It uses the Web Crypto API (PBKDF2 and AES-GCM). The page does not send the text or the passphrase to NeoNema.

NeoNema does not collect, store, or transmit what you type, the passphrase, or the encrypted result. There are no accounts, no cookies set by this tool, no analytics, and no advertising. A lost passphrase cannot be recovered, because it never leaves your device.

If you copy a result, your browser places it on the device clipboard. NeoNema cannot read that clipboard.

This site is served from a content delivery network, which processes standard request data (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver the page — the same as any website you visit. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. No other third-party services are used.
