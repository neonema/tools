Privacy Policy

The sensitivity calculator at tools.neonema.com converts a DPI and an in-game sensitivity into centimeters per 360° and the matching sensitivity in other games. The math runs entirely in your browser on your device.

We do not collect, store, or transmit the game, DPI, or sensitivity you enter. There are no accounts, no cookies set by this tool, no analytics, and no advertising.

This site is served from a content delivery network, which processes standard request data (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver the page — the same as any website you visit. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. No other third-party services are used.
